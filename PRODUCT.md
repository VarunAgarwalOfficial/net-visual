# Product

net-visual is a set of explorable simulations for a university computer
networks course. They will be hosted on the course tutor's personal website.

## Users

- Students, on laptops and phones, revising a protocol before a tutorial or
  an exam. They need to see why a protocol behaves as it does, not only what
  it does.
- The tutor, presenting in class from a projector. He needs presentation
  mode, links to exact moments, and scenarios he can rebuild quickly.

## Register

Scholarly and quiet, in the manner of a well-typeset textbook. Computer
Modern type, lettered algorithm steps in Knuth's style, mathematics in KaTeX,
and colour only where it carries meaning. The tools are where you work and the
article is what you read. The site's design tokens govern both.

## Principles

- The model is real and deterministic. Never show a canned animation.
- Every formula shown is computed from the current settings and set against
  the simulation.
- State the simplifications in the interface.
- Direct manipulation comes first: click a packet to edit it, and drag to
  delay it.
- Keyboard, phone, light and dark are all first-class.
